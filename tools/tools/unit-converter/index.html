About the Unit Converter

The Unit Converter on Calcify is a free, browser-based tool that converts any value between more than 200 units across 20 practical categories — length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data storage, pressure, energy, power, angle, fuel efficiency, frequency and cooking measurements. Whether you are a student solving a physics problem, an engineer checking specifications, a chef scaling a recipe from cups to grams, a traveller comparing kilometres to miles, or a developer converting bytes to gigabytes, this is the only converter you will ever need. Every conversion runs instantly inside your browser as you type — no submit button, no page reload, no waiting for a server response. The moment you change a number or select a new unit, the answer appears in the result panel.

Unit conversion is one of those everyday tasks that quietly trips people up. Do you multiply or divide when moving from inches to centimetres? Is a UK gallon the same as a US gallon? How many ounces are in a cup — and does that change for flour versus water? Does 100 Mbps equal 12.5 MB/s or 100 MB/s? Getting any of these wrong by a factor of 8 or 10 has real consequences: a recipe that fails, a project that misses spec, a purchase that does not fit. Calcify's Unit Converter uses internationally recognised conversion factors for every category, including the tricky distinctions between US and UK customary units, binary and decimal data-storage units, and precise temperature formulas. You get the right answer, every time.

Beyond the primary conversion, the tool is genuinely informative. It shows you the formula being used, the calculation step by step, and a full reference table that displays your value expressed in every unit of the current category at once. This is perfect for students who want to understand the underlying relationship between units, for professionals who need to cross-check a value against multiple measurement systems at a glance, and for anyone who simply wants to build intuition about how units relate. Common conversions are available as one-tap quick presets — for example "cm → inches", "kg → lbs" or "°C → °F" — so the most frequently used conversions are always a single click away.

The Unit Converter runs entirely inside your browser using plain JavaScript. No data is uploaded to any server, no signup is required, and there are no limits on how many conversions you can run. It works beautifully on phones, tablets and desktops, supports keyboard navigation and screen readers, and remembers your last used category so you can pick up exactly where you left off. Whether you are at your desk, in a lab, in a kitchen or on the road, this tool delivers instant, accurate, private unit conversions — completely free, forever.

How to use the Unit Converter

  1. 1

    Choose a category

    At the top of the calculator you will see horizontal tabs for each of the 20+ categories — Length, Weight, Temperature, Area, Volume, Speed, Time, Data Storage, Pressure, Energy, Power, Angle, Fuel Efficiency, Frequency and Cooking. Tap the one that matches your need. Each tab has its own set of units and a dedicated conversion formula, so you always get category-correct results.

  2. 2

    Enter your value

    Type the number you want to convert into the Value field. Decimal numbers are fully supported, and you can use negative numbers for temperature conversions below zero. The calculator updates live as you type — you do not need to press Enter or any button. If you make a mistake, just edit the number and the result refreshes instantly.

  3. 3

    Pick your "from" and "to" units

    Use the two dropdowns — From and To — to select the units you are converting between. Both dropdowns show every available unit for the chosen category, with abbreviations and full names so there is no ambiguity. If you want to flip the conversion (for example, converting metres to feet instead of feet to metres), tap the swap button in the middle and the values reverse automatically.

  4. 4

    Read the result and formula

    Your converted value appears in the highlighted card, with the correct unit name below it. Underneath, the conversion formula shows exactly how the result was calculated — including the conversion factor used. This is invaluable if you want to verify the result, teach someone how the conversion works, or understand the relationship between the two units rather than just accepting a number.

  5. 5

    Compare across all units and copy

    A reference table below the formula lists your value converted into every unit in the current category — handy for seeing the same measurement in multiple systems at once (for example, 100 cm alongside metres, millimetres, inches, feet and miles). When you find the value you need, hit "Copy result" to save the full breakdown to your clipboard, ready to paste into a document, spreadsheet or chat.

Frequently asked questions

The seven questions people ask us most about unit conversion.

Which unit categories does this converter support?

Calcify's Unit Converter supports 15 major categories and more than 200 individual units: Length (metre, centimetre, kilometre, mile, inch, foot, yard, nautical mile and more), Weight and Mass (kilogram, gram, pound, ounce, stone, tonne), Temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin), Area (square metre, square foot, acre, hectare), Volume (litre, millilitre, gallon US/UK, cup, pint, fluid ounce), Speed (km/h, mph, m/s, knots), Time (second, minute, hour, day, week, year), Data Storage (byte, KB, MB, GB, TB, PB — both binary and decimal), Pressure (Pascal, bar, PSI, atmosphere), Energy (joule, calorie, kilocalorie, kWh), Power (watt, kilowatt, horsepower), Angle (degree, radian, gradian), Fuel Efficiency (km/litre, mpg US/UK, litre/100km), Frequency (hertz, kilohertz, megahertz, gigahertz) and Cooking measurements.

How accurate are the conversions?

Every conversion uses internationally recognised standard factors — the same ones used in scientific research, engineering specifications and commercial trade. For length and mass, we use exact SI definitions (for example, 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly). For temperature we use the precise formulas that account for the offset, not just the scaling ratio. For data storage, we support both decimal (1 KB = 1,000 bytes) and binary (1 KiB = 1,024 bytes) systems because both are used in different contexts. The calculator displays results with up to six significant figures to balance accuracy and readability.

What is the difference between US and UK units?

Several units differ between the US customary system and the UK imperial system, even though they share the same names. The most common ones: a US gallon is 3.785 litres while a UK gallon is 4.546 litres (about 20% bigger). A US fluid ounce is 29.57 ml while a UK fluid ounce is 28.41 ml. A US pint is 473 ml while a UK pint is 568 ml. Stone (14 pounds) is commonly used in the UK for body weight but rarely in the US. Our converter lets you pick US or UK variants for gallons, pints, fluid ounces and fuel efficiency, so you always know which system you are working with.

How do I convert between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin?

Temperature conversions use formulas, not simple multiplication, because the scales have different zero points. Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Celsius to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15. Kelvin to Celsius: °C = K − 273.15. Fahrenheit to Kelvin: K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15. Our calculator applies the correct formula automatically — just select the temperature category and choose your source and target units. It handles negative values (like −40 °C, which happens to equal −40 °F) and extreme values correctly.

Is this Unit Converter free and private?

Yes — completely free with no signup, no email verification and no limit on how many conversions you can run. Every calculation happens inside your browser using plain JavaScript, so the values you type are never sent to any server, never stored, and never shared with any third party. You can even use the converter offline once the page has loaded. It is a genuinely private tool that you can trust with any measurement — whether it is a personal body weight, a confidential project spec, or a simple recipe quantity.

What is the difference between decimal and binary data units?

Decimal units (kB, MB, GB) are based on powers of 1000: 1 kilobyte = 1,000 bytes, 1 megabyte = 1,000,000 bytes. Binary units (KiB, MiB, GiB) are based on powers of 1024: 1 kibibyte = 1,024 bytes. Hard drive manufacturers use decimal units, which is why a "500 GB" drive shows up as about 465 GiB in your operating system. Our converter lets you convert between both systems accurately, so you can see exactly how many binary gigabytes are in a decimal terabyte. This matters for storage planning, bandwidth calculations, and understanding why your "1 TB" drive does not show 1,000 GB in Windows.

Does the converter work on mobile phones?

Yes, absolutely. The Unit Converter is built mobile-first and works flawlessly on any screen size. On smaller screens, the category tabs scroll horizontally so you can still reach every category with a single swipe. The value input uses the correct numeric keyboard for fast typing, and the dropdowns are large enough to tap comfortably. You can add the page to your phone home screen for one-tap access, and it will keep working even without an internet connection once loaded. Whether you are in a shop, a lab, a kitchen or on the road, the converter is always a tap away.
