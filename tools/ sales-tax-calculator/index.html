About the Sales Tax Calculator

The Sales Tax Calculator on Calcify is a free, browser-based tool that lets you add sales tax to a base price or remove it from a gross amount in one click. Sales tax is charged on the sale of goods and services in almost every country, though the name and rate vary widely — the US calls it sales tax (ranging from 0% in states like Oregon and New Hampshire to over 10% in parts of California and Louisiana), the UK calls it VAT at 20%, Canada uses a mix of GST, HST and PST ranging from 5% to 15%, Australia charges a flat 10% GST, and India uses GST at multiple slabs of 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. Whatever your country and rate, our calculator applies the correct formula instantly and shows you the exact base price, tax amount and total in your local currency. Whether you are a small business owner preparing an invoice, an accountant reconciling receipts, a student learning how tax works, or a shopper trying to understand how much tax is buried inside a price, this tool gives you a clear, accurate breakdown in seconds.

The tool supports two distinct modes. In Add Tax mode, you enter the net price before tax and the calculator shows you the tax amount and the final gross price the customer pays — this is the mode you use when quoting a price to a buyer or preparing a sales invoice. In Remove Tax mode (also called reverse sales tax calculation), you enter the gross price the customer has paid, and the calculator extracts the base price and the tax component that was already included — this is the mode you use when a customer gives you only the total, when you are reconciling receipts, or when you need to separate the tax from the sale amount for accounting and tax filings. Both modes are handled by the exact same formula, applied in the correct direction, and the result updates live as you type.

Beyond the numbers, the calculator is genuinely useful for daily work. Common tax rates appear as one-tap chips — 5%, 7%, 10%, 15%, 18%, 20% and 25% — so you can apply the most frequently used rates without typing anything. For any rate outside that list, you simply type it into the custom field. The built-in multi-currency support means the calculator works with USD, INR, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, AED and SAR, formatting every result with the correct local symbol and comma style — so a US user sees $107.00, an Indian user sees ₹107.00, and a UK user sees £107.00, all with the same clean formula. Every calculation runs entirely inside your browser using plain JavaScript, so no data is sent to any server. Your prices, invoices and tax figures remain completely private. The tool works perfectly on phones, tablets and desktops, supports dark mode, four languages and right-to-left layout, and is free to use as many times as you like — no signup, no limits, no hidden costs.

Two Simple Formulas

Add Tax: Total = Base × (1 + Rate ÷ 100)
Remove Tax: Base = Gross ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100)

How to use the Sales Tax Calculator

  1. 1

    Choose your currency

    Select your preferred currency from the dropdown at the top of the input panel — USD, INR, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, AED or SAR. All input fields, results and formatting update instantly to use the correct symbol and comma style for your currency. This makes the calculator feel native whether you are in New York, New Delhi, London, Toronto or Dubai.

  2. 2

    Pick Add or Remove mode

    Use Add Tax when you have a base (pre-tax) price and want to know the final amount with tax added — perfect for preparing invoices and quotes. Use Remove Tax when you have a total (tax-inclusive) price and want to extract the base and the tax — perfect for accounting, reconciliation and receipt analysis. The input label and hint text change automatically to remind you which number to enter.

  3. 3

    Enter amount and pick rate

    Type the price into the amount field. Then click one of the seven rate chips — 5%, 7%, 10%, 15%, 18%, 20% or 25% — for the most common tax slabs. If your rate is not in the list (for example 6.25% in Massachusetts, 8.875% in New York City, or 13% HST in Ontario), simply type it into the "Custom rate" field. The result updates live on every keystroke, so you can experiment with different rates and see how the tax amount changes.

  4. 4

    Read the breakdown

    The result panel shows the total price prominently along with the base amount and the tax amount as separate cards. Below, the step-by-step working panel shows exactly how the numbers were calculated, so you can verify the math yourself or explain it to a customer or colleague. In Remove mode, the total card shows the extracted base price, and the subtext shows what the original total was and how much tax was inside it.

  5. 5

    Copy the result and use it anywhere

    When you are happy with the calculation, click "Copy result" and the full breakdown — mode, amount, rate, base price, tax amount and total — is copied to your clipboard in a clean, readable format. Paste it into an invoice, an email, a chat message, WhatsApp, a spreadsheet or your accounting software. Nothing you type is stored anywhere — the entire calculation happens inside your browser, so your prices and tax figures remain completely private.
