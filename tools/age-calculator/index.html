How It Works: Mathematical Formula & Principles

Computing exact chronological age differs from simple calendar subtraction because individual months contain differing day counts (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and solar years encompass leap days:

Age = TargetDate − BirthDate
Years = Y₂ − Y₁ − (if M₂ < M₁ or (M₂ == M₁ and D₂ < D₁) then 1 else 0)
Remaining Days = D₂ − D₁ (with borrow from preceding month length)

When the target day number is smaller than the birth day number, the calculator borrows the exact number of days in the month prior to the target month, ensuring full calendar accuracy without rounding errors.
