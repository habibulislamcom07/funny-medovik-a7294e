How It Works: The Quetelet BMI Formula

Body Mass Index was developed in the 19th century by Belgian mathematician Adolphe Quetelet and remains the clinical standard benchmark worldwide:

Metric Formula: BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²

Imperial Formula: BMI = 703 × [Weight (lbs)] / [Height (inches)]²

For example, an adult weighing 70 kilograms with a height of 175 centimeters (1.75 meters) has a BMI of: 70 / (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.86 kg/m², residing comfortably in the healthy normal range.
