About the Word Counter

The Word Counter on Calcify is a free, browser-based tool that gives you instant, accurate counts of everything in your text — words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, reading time, speaking time and keyword density. Whether you are a student working on an essay with a strict word limit, a blogger optimising a post for SEO, a marketer drafting a tweet or LinkedIn post, a content writer checking readability, or just curious how long your message really is, this is the only counter you need. Everything updates live as you type — no submit button, no page reload, no waiting. The moment you pause for a breath, the numbers are already in front of you.

Word counting sounds trivial until you actually need it done correctly. Different apps count words differently. Microsoft Word splits hyphenated words one way, Google Docs another, and some social media platforms strip out emojis, URLs or even @mentions before counting. If you are writing a 250-word college application essay, a 2,000-word SEO blog post, or a 280-character tweet, being one word or one character over the limit can mean a rejected submission or a truncated post. Our Word Counter uses a careful word-splitting algorithm that handles punctuation, contractions, numbers, acronyms and hyphens the same way professional writing tools do, so you can trust the number you see here matches the number your teacher, editor or platform expects.

Beyond the basic counts, the tool gives you genuinely useful extra insights. The reading time estimate uses the industry-standard 200 words per minute average and helps you understand how long a visitor will spend reading your article — a metric every content creator should know. The speaking time estimate (at 130 words per minute) is perfect for preparing a script for a YouTube video, podcast or presentation. The social media character limits panel shows at a glance whether your text fits within Twitter/X (280), Instagram captions (2,200), LinkedIn (3,000) or Facebook (63,206) — with colour-coded indicators that change as you approach each limit. And the keyword density analyser shows you the most frequently used words in your text so you can check that your writing is naturally optimised for SEO without crossing into keyword stuffing.

The Word Counter runs entirely inside your browser using plain JavaScript. Nothing you type is ever sent to any server, stored in any database or shared with any third party. That makes it safe to use for private drafts, confidential business content, unpublished articles, legal documents and anything else you would never want to paste into a cloud service. It works flawlessly on phones, tablets and desktops, supports keyboard shortcuts, respects dark mode and remembers nothing about you between visits. Whether you are a writer, student, marketer, editor or professional, this is the cleanest, fastest, most private Word Counter you will find — and it will always be free.

How to use the Word Counter

  1. 1

    Paste or type your text

    Click into the large text area and start typing, or paste text copied from anywhere — Word documents, Google Docs, emails, PDFs, web pages or your phone's clipboard. There is no character limit, so you can comfortably process full-length articles, essays, reports or book chapters. Use the Paste button if your browser blocks the standard paste shortcut for security reasons.

  2. 2

    Watch the live stats update

    The six stat tiles at the top — Words, Characters, No Spaces, Sentences, Paragraphs and Reading Time — update in real time as you type. There is no submit button and no refresh delay. You will see the numbers tick up the instant you add or remove content, which makes it easy to trim an essay down to an exact limit or expand a post until it hits a target length.

  3. 3

    Check reading time and speaking time

    The highlighted card shows the estimated reading time (based on 200 words per minute) and speaking time (based on 130 words per minute). Reading time is essential for bloggers and SEO writers who want to match visitor expectations. Speaking time is essential for content creators preparing YouTube scripts, podcast episodes, presentations or speeches — you will know exactly how many minutes of content you have written before you record.

  4. 4

    Verify social media limits

    The "Social Media Character Limits" panel shows your current count against the maximum allowed on Twitter/X (280), Instagram captions (2,200), LinkedIn posts (3,000), Facebook posts (63,206), SEO meta titles (60) and meta descriptions (160). The colours change as you approach each limit — green means safe, amber means close to the edge, and red means you are over. Perfect for preparing posts and meta tags before you publish.

  5. 5

    Analyse keywords and copy the result

    The "Top Keywords" card shows the most frequently used words in your text along with their count and percentage density. Use this to check that your SEO focus keyword appears a natural number of times (typically 1–2% density) without ever tipping into keyword stuffing — which search engines penalise. When you are finished, use the Copy button to copy your text to the clipboard, or the Clear button to start fresh.

Frequently asked questions

The seven questions people ask us most about word counting.

How does the Word Counter count words?

The counter splits your text using whitespace boundaries (spaces, tabs, line breaks) and filters out empty strings. Punctuation, numbers and contractions like "don't" or "it's" are treated the same way professional tools like Microsoft Word and Google Docs treat them — so the count you see here matches what your teacher, editor or publishing platform expects. Hyphenated words such as "state-of-the-art" are counted as one word, matching standard writing conventions.

Does the Word Counter count characters with and without spaces?

Yes — you get both counts. "Characters" shows the total including spaces, tabs and line breaks, which is what most social media platforms and SMS limit you on. "Characters (No Spaces)" shows the total with all whitespace removed, which is what SEO meta descriptions, title tags and some application forms require. Both numbers update live as you type, so you always know exactly where you stand.

How is reading time and speaking time calculated?

Reading time is calculated using the industry-standard average of 200 words per minute for adult readers of English. Speaking time uses an average of 130 words per minute, which is typical for natural speech in presentations, YouTube videos, podcasts and speeches. These figures match the estimates used by Medium, WordPress plugins and most SEO tools. If you speak faster or slower than average, adjust the numbers by 10-15% either way.

Is this Word Counter free and private?

Yes — completely free with no signup, no email requirement and no limit on how many times you use it. The most important part: nothing you type ever leaves your browser. There is no server, no database, no logging and no analytics on the text itself. That makes it safe for private drafts, confidential business documents, unpublished articles and anything else you would never paste into a cloud service. You can even use it offline once the page has loaded.

Can I count words for social media posts?

Absolutely. The "Social Media Character Limits" panel shows your count against Twitter/X (280), Instagram captions (2,200), LinkedIn posts (3,000), Facebook posts (63,206), SEO meta titles (60) and meta descriptions (160). The colour changes from green to amber to red as you approach each limit, so you can trim or expand your text before you hit publish. This is especially useful for Twitter/X where a single extra character means your tweet cannot send.

What is keyword density and why does it matter?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a specific word or phrase appears in your text relative to the total number of words. For most SEO content, a target keyword density of 1-2% is ideal — enough for search engines to understand what your content is about, without crossing into "keyword stuffing" which modern algorithms penalise. The "Top Keywords" panel shows the most frequent words in your text with their exact counts and percentage, so you can check your focus keyword's density instantly without installing any SEO plugin.

Does the Word Counter work on mobile and offline?

Yes to both. The Word Counter is built mobile-first and works beautifully on any screen size from a small budget phone to a 4K desktop monitor. All calculations run in your browser, so once the page has fully loaded, it continues to work perfectly even without an internet connection — great for writing on flights, trains or anywhere with poor signal. You can also add the page to your phone's home screen for one-tap access, giving you a permanent word-counting tool in your pocket.
