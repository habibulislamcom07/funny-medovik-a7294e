How It Works: GST Mathematical Formulas

Goods and Services Tax is an ad valorem indirect tax. The formulas depend on whether the initial transaction figure includes or excludes tax:

Exclusive GST (Adding Tax):
GST Amount = (Net Base × GST Rate %) / 100
Gross Total = Net Base + GST Amount

Inclusive GST (Removing Tax):
Net Base = Gross Total / [1 + (GST Rate % / 100)]
GST Amount = Gross Total − Net Base

Central & State Apportionment:
CGST = GST Amount / 2
SGST = GST Amount / 2

For cross-border inter-state shipments, Integrated GST (IGST) equals 100% of the total GST amount without the CGST/SGST split.
