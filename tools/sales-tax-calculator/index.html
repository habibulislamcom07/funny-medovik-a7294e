Sales Tax Formulas Explained

Sales tax is an ad valorem tax applied at point of purchase. Calculation formulas vary depending on tax inclusion:

Adding Sales Tax (Exclusive):
Sales Tax Amount = Net Base Price × (Sales Tax Rate / 100)
Total Retail Cost = Net Base Price + Sales Tax Amount

Removing Sales Tax (Inclusive):
Pre-Tax Base Price = Total Retail Cost / [1 + (Sales Tax Rate / 100)]
Sales Tax Amount = Total Retail Cost − Pre-Tax Base Price

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Tax Exclusive means sales tax is added on top of the base sticker price at checkout. Tax Inclusive means the final price already includes sales tax, and the tool calculates the net pre-tax amount.
