What is Sales Tax?

Sales tax is a consumption tax imposed by state and local governments on the retail sale of goods and specific services. Unlike Value Added Tax (VAT), sales tax is levied only once at the final point of purchase when a consumer buys a product.

In the United States, individual states, counties, and municipal cities set their own specific sales tax rates. Total sales tax usually includes a statewide base rate combined with local city or county surcharges.

Sales Tax Formulas

Adding Sales Tax (Exclusive):
Tax Amount = Net Base Price × (Tax Rate / 100)
Total Cost = Net Base Price + Tax Amount

Removing Sales Tax (Inclusive):
Pre-Tax Price = Total Cost / [1 + (Tax Rate / 100)]
Tax Amount = Total Cost − Pre-Tax Price
