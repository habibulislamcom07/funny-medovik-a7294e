Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains in plain English what data Calcify collects (very little), how we use it, and what rights you have over your information.

Last updated: 15 January 2025 Reading time: 7 minutes GDPR & CCPA aware

Welcome to Calcify ("we," "us," "our"). Calcify operates the website at https://calcify.com/ and provides 150+ free online calculators and utility tools. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or use any of our tools. By using Calcify, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our site.

The Short Version

All calculations on Calcify run entirely inside your browser. We never receive, store, or see the numbers, dates, text, or any other values you enter into our tools. We only collect anonymous analytics data (page views, device type, country) and use third-party advertising cookies to keep the site free.

1. Information We Collect

We collect two types of information when you use Calcify: information you voluntarily provide to us (very limited) and information automatically collected by analytics and advertising services.

1.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

The only time you provide personal information to us is when you:

  • Send us an email at mediaprojects.official@gmail.com — we receive your email address and whatever content you choose to share.
  • Subscribe to our newsletter — we collect your email address so we can send you the weekly tool digest. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Contact us through a contact form (if available) — we receive the name, email, and message you submit.

We never ask for your name, phone number, address, payment card, government ID, or any sensitive personal information to use our tools. Ever.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit Calcify, we and our third-party service providers (Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Vercel) may collect the following anonymous technical information:

  • Device information — browser type and version, operating system, screen size, device type (mobile/tablet/desktop).
  • Usage data — the pages you visit, time spent on each page, referring URL, and click patterns.
  • Approximate location — country and city derived from your IP address (we never store the full IP address for longer than 30 days).
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small text files placed on your device to remember preferences and serve relevant ads.

This information is aggregated and anonymous — it cannot be used to personally identify you.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

To be completely transparent, Calcify never collects:

  • The numbers, dates, text, or any values you enter into our calculators and tools.
  • Your browsing history outside of Calcify.
  • Your name, address, or phone number.
  • Your payment information (we have no paid features that require it for the free tools).
  • Your precise GPS location.
  • Your biometric or health data (BMI, BMR, etc. inputs stay on your device).

2. How We Use Your Information

The limited information we collect is used for the following purposes only:

  • To operate and improve Calcify — understanding which tools are popular helps us build new ones you actually need.
  • To serve relevant advertising — our ad partners use cookies to display ads that match your interests, which keeps the site free for everyone.
  • To respond to your requests — if you email us or subscribe, we use your email to reply or send newsletters.
  • To maintain security — detecting and preventing abuse, fraud, and attacks on our servers.
  • To comply with the law — where required by legal process or regulation.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not spam, and we do not share email addresses with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Calcify uses cookies and similar technologies (local storage, pixel tags) to deliver a better experience. A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device. Cookies help us remember your preferences (like dark mode and language), analyse site traffic, and serve relevant advertising.

3.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function. For example, remembering your theme preference (dark or light) and language selection. Without these, the site would reset on every page load.
  • Analytics cookies — set by Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. These cookies collect aggregated anonymous data such as page views, session duration, and bounce rate. Google Analytics anonymises IP addresses before storage.
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google AdSense and its partners to serve personalised ads based on your browsing behaviour. These cookies may track you across multiple websites to build an interest profile. You can opt out at any time via the links below.
  • Preference cookies — store your chosen language and last-used tool category so the site feels native on your next visit.

3.2 Managing Cookies

You have full control over cookies. You can:

  • Block all cookies in your browser settings — but this may break some site features like dark mode persistence.
  • Opt out of personalised Google Ads by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics using the official browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Use your browser's incognito/private mode for a cookie-free experience.
  • Clear cookies any time from your browser's settings panel.

4. Third-Party Services

Calcify uses several trusted third-party services to operate and monetise the site. Each of these may collect anonymous data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdSense — our primary advertising partner. AdSense and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Google Analytics — provides anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the site. Data is aggregated and never personally identifies you. Read more at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider. Vercel may log IP addresses and request metadata for security and abuse prevention purposes. Read their privacy policy at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Cloudflare / CDN providers — may cache static files and log requests for DDoS protection and performance.
  • Affiliate partners — Amazon Associates, Flipkart Affiliate, and Hostinger. When you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, they may set a cookie to attribute the sale to Calcify. This never affects the price you pay.

We choose our partners carefully and only work with companies that respect user privacy and comply with major data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, PECR).

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have specific legal rights over your personal information. Calcify respects all of the following:

5.1 GDPR Rights (EU, UK, Switzerland)

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — you can ask us to delete your data.
  • Right to restrict processing — you can limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — you can request your data in a portable format.
  • Right to object — you can object to processing based on legitimate interest or direct marketing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority.

5.2 CCPA Rights (California, USA)

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the "sale" of personal information. Calcify does not sell personal information.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

5.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the above rights, email us at mediaprojects.official@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request". We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for exercising your privacy rights.

6. Children's Privacy

Calcify is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at mediaprojects.official@gmail.com, and we will delete the information promptly.

7. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Calcify uses industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption — all data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted with TLS 1.3.
  • Client-side calculations — all calculator logic runs in your browser, so no calculation data ever reaches our servers.
  • Minimal data storage — we keep only the bare minimum information needed to operate the site.
  • Regular updates — our dependencies are kept up to date with the latest security patches.
  • Secure hosting — we host on Vercel, which maintains SOC 2 Type II compliance.

That said, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we do our best to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have concerns about transmitting any information to us, please use a secure network and avoid sharing sensitive data over email.

8. International Data Transfers

Calcify is operated from India but is available globally. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own (including the United States and the European Union) where our service providers operate data centres. When we transfer data internationally, we rely on standard contractual clauses and other legal safeguards approved by the European Commission to ensure your data receives adequate protection.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Post a notice on our home page for at least 7 days.
  • Notify newsletter subscribers by email if the changes are significant.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of Calcify after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us:

Email
mediaprojects.official@gmail.com

We aim to respond to all privacy-related emails within 30 days. For urgent security matters, please mention "URGENT" in the subject line.

Thanks for using Calcify. We built it with privacy first.
