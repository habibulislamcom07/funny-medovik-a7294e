Frequently Asked Questions

Before you send us a message, check whether your question is already answered below. These are the most common enquiries we receive every week.

How quickly will I get a reply?

We aim to reply to every message within 1-2 business days. Simple questions and bug reports usually get a faster response. If your message is about a critical security issue, please mention "URGENT" in the subject line and we will prioritise it.

Can I request a new calculator or tool?

Absolutely — we love tool requests from our users. Many of the 150+ calculators on Calcify were built because someone asked for them. Please use the contact form with the subject "New Tool Request" and describe exactly what you want the tool to do. If it fits our categories and gets enough interest, we will build it.

I found a bug or calculation error. What should I do?

Please email us with the subject "Bug Report" and include: the exact tool name, the values you entered, the result you got, the result you expected, and the source for your expected result (for example, a bank statement, medical guideline, or standard reference). This lets us fix the issue quickly and accurately. We take every bug report seriously.

Is my data safe when I submit the contact form?

Yes. The only data we store from a contact form submission is your name, email address, subject and message — used solely to reply to you. We never sell, share or use your email for marketing. Your data is protected under our Privacy Policy and you can request deletion at any time by writing to us again.

Do you accept advertising or sponsorships?

Yes — we work with carefully chosen partners whose products genuinely help our users. If you are interested in advertising, sponsorship, or content collaboration, please contact us with the subject "Advertising Enquiry" and include details about your product, your target audience and your budget. We do not accept ads that are misleading, deceptive or that violate Google's advertising policies.

Can I contribute or collaborate with Calcify?

We are always open to collaboration with writers, developers, designers and content creators who share our vision of fast, private, accurate tools. Send us a message with the subject "Partnership / Business" describing how you would like to work with us, and include links to your portfolio or previous work. We review every proposal personally.

How do I report a privacy or legal concern?

For any privacy, copyright, trademark or legal matter, please email us with the subject "Privacy or Legal". Include your full name, contact details, the specific page or tool involved, and a clear description of your concern. We respond to all legal enquiries within 30 days as outlined in our Terms of Use.

Support Commitment

Every message lands in the same inbox — there is no automated bot, no ticket queue, no "priority tier". A real human reads your email and replies personally. Whether you are reporting a bug, requesting a tool, or just saying thanks, you will hear back from a real person.

Prefer Email Instead?

If you would rather skip the form and email us directly, that is completely fine. Write to us at:

mediaprojects.official@gmail.com

Using the form is faster because it automatically includes the subject line and structured information, but direct email works perfectly too.
